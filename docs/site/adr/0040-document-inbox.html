  • Implements product ADR-007 (upload without binding to a year, assignment as a suggestion), product ADR-008 (line-item conflict visible instead of silent overwriting)

  • Context tags: architecture, receipt inbox, security, honesty principle

#Context

Product ADR-007: users photograph receipts at the moment of receipt, not while filing — so a Beleg (document) lands unbound (no year); extraction suggests a year (payment date over invoice date, § 11 EStG Zufluss/Abfluss (inflow/outflow)) and a tap confirms. Product ADR-008: conflicting sources become a visible conflict, never a silent overwrite. Scan/OCR is out of scope here (ADR-036/045, later slices) — this slice builds the data interface only.

#Decision

  1. packages/core/beleg.ts — the pure model:

    • BelegDaten: the persisted facts (id, userId, filename, optional invoice/payment dates, confirmed year).
    • steuerjahrVorschlag: the § 11 rule — payment date's year, else invoice date's year, else none; carries the herkunft (zahlungsdatum|belegdatum) for the "Regel: Zahlungsdatum" ("Rule: payment date") chip.
    • zuordnungAusDaten: the derived state (ungebunden | vorgeschlagen | zugeordnet | konflikt). konflikt = a confirmed year contradicts the date-derived suggestion — both years stay visible (ADR-008).
    • ordneZu: the one assignment operation; it persists the year, and the conflict is derived on the next read, never a separate stored flag.

    The Zuordnung (assignment) state is derived, never stored — same instinct as TaxYear's range being recomputed (ADR-014/048): a stored copy of a derivable state could only drift.

  2. apps/api Beleg module + Prisma migration — POST /v1/belege (unbound create), GET /v1/belege (inbox), POST /v1/belege/:id/zuordnung (the tap). Guest-compatible (UserContextGuard, like Profile/Cockpit — receipts are collected "im Moment des Erhalts" (at the moment of receipt), possibly pre-account; REQ-006 carries them over). The Beleg table stores facts; dateiname is field-encrypted at rest (ADR-0008 — filenames carry PII, joinable to Profile's Steuer-ID (tax identification number)); dates stay clear (the suggestion rule and the (userId, steuerjahr) index need them without decrypting). WRITE/READ access is audited (REQ-004).

#Consequences

  • The conflict is honest and cheap: assigning a year that contradicts the dates just works and the derived state reports konflikt — nothing is silently overwritten, no extra column.
  • The Posten-amount conflict (ADR-008's "Beleg sagt 480 €, Nutzer tippt 500 €" — document says 480 €, user types 500 €) lives in the calculation core (PostenKonflikt, draft 0038) because it is triggered by OCR, which is not built yet — this slice's Beleg table therefore stores no betrag column (honest: no amount to store).
  • Security posture: new persistent user data. dateiname field-encrypted (ADR-0008); access audited (REQ-004). The receipt binary storage is out of scope here (ADR-036/045) — when it lands, the storage-key field must join the encrypted set.
  • Open for promotion review: whether the "conflict on the year" is even a product-visible state, or whether ADR-008's conflict is exclusively the Posten-amount conflict and the year reassignment should simply overwrite (the slice modeled the year-conflict because the ticket AC lists ADR-007/008 together — a product call, not a settled law).

#Alternatives considered

  • Store the Zuordnung state as a column. Rejected: a derivable state is exactly what ADR-014/048 says to recompute, never persist.
  • Require @RequiresAccount() (no guest). Rejected: receipts are photographed at receipt time, possibly before account; Profile already establishes guest-compatible sensitive persistence (field-encrypted, REQ-006 carries it over).
