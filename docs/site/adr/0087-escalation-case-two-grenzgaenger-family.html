Decision · ADR-0087

Escalation case: two Grenzgänger with a change year, three children, medical costs Accepted

  • Date: 2026-11-14

#Context

A deliberately exaggerated, fictional case: both spouses work until June for a German employer (German social insurance and pension) and from July as Grenzgänger (cross-border commuters) in Switzerland (AHV, Pensionskasse with Obligatorium, Quellensteuer). Three children are in kindergarten and day care, there is a dentist invoice of €1,700 and a cosmetic surgery. Every document should find a field, and the printout should be complete. The first run found these gaps:

  • For Person B there was neither a Lohnausweis (Swiss salary certificate) form nor an interface. B's employer address, rate and Grenzgänger status could not be entered anywhere; the printout stayed locked.
  • The joint estimate did not count B's Swiss salary as soon as B also had an LStB, because B as a person without an account has no interview answer „Schweiz“ ("Switzerland").
  • B's read LStB was prepared as a draft for A and displaced A's own LStB drafts.
  • Amounts with a thousands dot were read incorrectly (€1.700,00 as €700).
  • Doctor and dentist invoices found no field. A cosmetic surgery was not recognized.
  • The self-borne costs in the Swiss health insurance statement appeared only as a hint.
  • A day care document with an annual total paid yielded only the period, not the amount.
  • An invoice that builds on a still open document draft replaced it instead of adding to it.

#Decision

  1. B's Swiss data. All endpoints of ch-lohnausweis take ?person=B, like LStB and commute (ADR-0054). B's own Grenzgänger questions (residence, non-return days) are stored by PUT ch-lohnausweis/grenzgaenger?person=B as B's answers. Estimate, printout and form derive B's status from them as for A from the interview. For A the interview remains the place for these questions (400 without person=B). In the cockpit, B has the field „Lohnausweis Schweiz“ in a Switzerland tax return. B's form first asks for residence and days.
  2. Estimate. A person with a Lohnausweis counts as a Grenzgänger if they answered „Schweiz“ or answered the Grenzgänger questions. With an LStB in addition it is a change year (ADR-0079), also for B.
  3. Whose LStB. An LStB without a specified person (upload in the chat) belongs, in a Zusammenveranlagung (joint assessment), to the person whose name is on it. If none matches, the chat asks as for the Lohnausweis. Drafts for B carry person: 'B' and do not displace A's drafts.
  4. Medical costs from invoices (§ 33 EStG). A doctor, dentist or similar invoice becomes a draft for gesundheit-betrag with „Ja“ ("Yes") as the accompanying answer. The amount is the own share after a printed reimbursement, otherwise the invoice amount. Several invoices add up, the same invoice counts once. The app does not record a cosmetic surgery or cosmetic treatment: no draft, and the chat says why (Product Owner decision: "do not record at all"). § 33 EStG is a legal source of the owl.
  5. Self-borne costs from the Swiss statement (franchise, deductible) go to the medical costs in euros at the Lohnausweis rate (otherwise at the annual average, „prüfen“ ("check")), once per statement.
  6. Day care amount. If a document states the total paid for the year („Gesamt 2025“, „im Jahr 2025 gezahlt“ ("total 2025", "paid in 2025")), it is proposed as a „prüfen“ draft. A monthly fee still does not count as an amount (ADR-0086).
  7. Summing up and conflicts. An invoice also adds onto an open document draft and takes over its counted items. A field that, when applying, already has exactly the draft's value (two documents both open „Krankheitskosten: Ja“ ("Medical costs: yes")) is not a stale state.
  8. Amounts with a thousands dot are read in full; the line with the invoice amount takes precedence.
  9. Reasonable burden with entered children. The interview answer „2 oder mehr“ ("2 or more") does not distinguish 2 from 3+ and still counts as "1–2". If children are entered for the year, their number decides: from three, 1/1/2 % applies (§ 33 Abs. 3 EStG). Found when comparing with the BMF calculator; its tariff values for the case matched.

#Consequences

  • Scenario 25 („Familie Sommer“) in the E2E matrix plays through the case: documents in the chat and via the same interface, B's Switzerland form in the browser, then the remaining forms. It checks the estimate with both change years, a printout without missing entries and every amount in its field. Medical costs: €1,700 plus the self-borne costs, never the €3,800 of the surgery.
  • Cosmetic surgeries with medical necessity confirmed by a public health officer are entered by the user themselves; the app does not recognize them as an exception.
  • The Anlage N still does not print the wage tax (it comes from the electronic LStB), only the gross salary.
