Decision · ADR-0043

Veranlagungsartenvergleich refinement: Soli/KiSt folded in, the uncertainty is now only the Progressionsvorbehalt Accepted

  • Context
  • Decision
  • Consequences
  • Alternatives considered
  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-10-24
  • Deciders: Robin (backend, ticket 10.2) — refinement of ADR-0034, triggered by Musti's review of the 10.2 branch (REQUEST-CHANGES F2).
  • Refines ADR-0034 Decision 3 (and its Consequences paragraph "until Soli and Kirchensteuer are computed …").
  • Applies ADR-0032 (no fabricated zero) and ADR-0033 § citation form — the PV bound is a new, citable derivation and belongs in the ADR log, not only in a module-header note.
  • Context tags: architecture, determinism boundary, honesty principle, tax computation

#Context

ADR-0034 defined the Veranlagungsartenvergleich (comparison of assessment types) as a bounded comparison and explicitly left three factors uncomputed — Solidaritätszuschlag (solidarity surcharge), church tax and Progressionsvorbehalt (progression proviso). Decision 3 modeled Soli and KiSt as a (computable) upper bound over numbers the module "already holds", and the Progressionsvorbehalt as unboundable: without input, unschaerfe is unbestimmt and aussage can never become eindeutig.

Ticket 10.2 (M1) closed two of these three gaps, and did so differently from how ADR-0034 had anticipated:

  1. Soli and KiSt are now computed (packages/core/src/tarif.ts — §4 SolZG with exemption limit + phase-in zone, §51a as a caller factor 0 | 0.08 | 0.09), and folded into einzel/zusammen/delta. ADR-0034's Decision 3 text ("an upper bound … arithmetic over numbers this module already holds") therefore describes a mechanism that no longer exists: Soli/KiSt are no longer part of unschaerfe.
  2. The Progressionsvorbehalt becomes boundable, via a new input object (ProgressionsvorbehaltEingabe) with a bound that ADR-0034 called non-derivable. The core invariant (bounded comparison, aussage first, empfehlung only on eindeutig) is fully preserved — this is a refinement, not a silent contradiction, and ADR-0034's Consequences paragraph ("until Soli and Kirchensteuer are computed and Progressionsvorbehalt is an input") anticipated exactly this step. But the ADR text on Decision 3 is now stale, and the new PV bound is a derivation that is missing from the ADR log (ADR-0033: citation form).

#Decision

Decision 3 of ADR-0034 is made more precise as follows (not rewritten retroactively — see Consequences):

  1. einzel/zusammen/delta carry the full assessed tax. The numbers are income tax plus Solidaritätszuschlag plus (optional) church tax — exact to the euro, for the two zvE (taxable income) bases passed in. Soli and KiSt are no longer part of the uncertainty: they are monotone surcharges on an income tax that differs between the assessment types, and cannot flip the sign of delta — an excluded factor that cannot tip the result does not belong in the error bound.

  2. unschaerfe is exclusively the Progressionsvorbehalt (§32b EStG). The only excluded factor that can tip the result. Its upper bound is 45 % × Lohnersatzleistungen (wage replacement benefits), rounded up to the whole euro (Math.ceil(MAX_GRENZSTEUERSATZ * betrag), MAX_GRENZSTEUERSATZ = 0.45 from §32a Abs. 1 Nr. 5 EStG). Derivation: the PV effect is y·[s(y+P)−s(y)] with s′(u)=(T′(u)−s(u))/u ≤ 45 %/u, hence ≤ 45 %·y·ln(1+P/y) ≤ 45 %·P; since the PV only increases both sides (einzel/zusammen), |Δdelta| ≤ 45 %·P holds. This is a conservative, citable upper limit — the top marginal tax rate as the ceiling, not a threshold chosen by feel (in the spirit of ADR-0034 "computed, not chosen").

  3. nicht-angegeben remains the honest indeterminate case. If the Progressionsvorbehalt is not (or not fully) captured — ProgressionsvorbehaltEingabe with art: 'nicht-angegeben' —, unschaerfe is unbestimmt and aussage can never become eindeutig. This also applies at the service level: a lohnersatz === 'Ja' without a captured (positive) amount is mapped to nicht-angegeben, not to lohnersatzleistungen with betrag: 0 (a fabricated zero would produce obergrenze: 0 and make a false eindeutig possible — ADR-0032).

aussage === 'eindeutig' if and only if unschaerfe.kind === 'bestimmt' && |delta| > unschaerfe.obergrenze — the same invariant as in ADR-0034 Decision 3, with the refined meaning of the two terms.

#Consequences

  • ADR-0034 remains the source of the invariant; Decision 3 there is no longer literally current. This ADR supersedes the Soli/KiSt bound part and the "PV is unboundable" part of ADR-0034 Decision 3. The rest of ADR-0034 (Decision 1 naming discipline, Decision 2 type enforcement, Decision 4 conclusion table) stays in force unchanged. ADR-0034 is not rewritten retroactively (log rule: an ADR that replaces another supersedes it, it does not rewrite history).
  • unschaerfe becomes narrower and more honest. Instead of a bound over three uncomputed factors, it is now the bound over exactly the one that can actually tip the result. The zu-knapp case is therefore wrong less often than under ADR-0034: a delta within the PV noise bound is no longer additionally burdened with the Soli/KiSt uncertainty.
  • The PV bound is a number someone must be able to defend. That is why it is stated here with its derivation, not as a comment in the module header. A caller reading unschaerfe.obergrenze can point to this ADR instead of justifying a constant.
  • Level 3 stays open. As in ADR-0034 §4, this module asserts nothing about the correctness of the zvE bases entered; in the M1 slice zveA/zveB are an income basis, not a real taxable income. This refinement does not change that.

#Alternatives considered

  • Keep modeling Soli/KiSt in unschaerfe instead of computing them. Rejected: ticket 10.2 computes them anyway (tarif.ts); treating them as an "uncomputable" bound would be a self-report about a factor the module now knows exactly, and would keep burdening einzel/ zusammen with an uncertainty that no longer exists.
  • Document the PV bound retroactively as part of ADR-0034 instead of creating a follow-up ADR. Rejected: the ADR log is append-only (4-digit, sequential, immutable); a retroactive change to Decision 3 would rewrite history. A follow-up ADR that references and refines is the rule.
  • Leave the PV bound as a comment in the module header. Rejected on ADR-0021's thesis: a note that nobody is required to read is not a control and not an ADR log entry (ADR-0033 citation form). The derivation belongs in the document that justifies architecture decisions.
