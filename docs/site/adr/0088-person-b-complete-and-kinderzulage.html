Decision · ADR-0088

Entering Person B completely, Swiss Kinderzulage per child Accepted

  • Context
  • Decision
  • Consequences
  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-14
  • Deciders: NexusHero (Product Owner), Steuereule Engineering
  • Applies to: packages/core/src/interview/interview.ts, packages/core/src/domains/{swiss-family-allowance,ch-salary-certificate-parser}.ts, packages/core/src/engine/married-estimate.ts, apps/api/src/{interview,pension,children,tax-estimate,printout,ch-certificate-scan}/, apps/api/src/advisor/advisor.service.ts, apps/api/prisma/, apps/mobile-web/src/screens/{catalog,children,cockpit,pension,interview}/, e2e/scenarios/26-familie-brandt-rente-und-zulage.mjs
  • Builds on: ADR-0054 (Person A enters data for B), ADR-0073 (drafts for B), ADR-0087 (escalation case)

#Context

After the escalation case (ADR-0087) gaps remained in a Zusammenveranlagung (joint assessment) in which A enters data for B:

  • Kinderzulage (Swiss child allowance): the allowance from B's employer was lost as soon as A had 0. Per child it was not possible to say who receives the allowance. The Lohnausweis (Swiss salary certificate) reader did not know any family allowances.
  • Pension: B's pension could not be entered. The joint estimate knew no pension at all and silently left out a retired partner.
  • Personal items: Riester, relocation or side job of B ended up in A's answers.
  • Interview: it asked nothing about B. The cockpit always showed B the same partner fields.
  • Tax rate: a Swiss salary without Grenzgänger (cross-border commuter) status was fully taxed, although it is exempt in Germany.
  • Documents from photos: individual document drafts carried no „prüfen“ ("check") marker.

#Decision

  1. The interview also asks about B. After „Verheiratet“ ("Married") it asks what Person B does (employed, retired, not working). For "employed" it additionally asks whether B works in Switzerland. The cockpit then shows only B's matching fields:

    • employed: LStB and commute, for Switzerland also the Lohnausweis;
    • retired: the pension field;
    • without an answer (older years): as before.

    B's residence and non-return days are asked only by B's Switzerland form; the engine knows no conditions across several answers.

  2. „Für wen?“ ("For whom?") in personal catalogs. In a Zusammenveranlagung, „Lebenslagen“ ("Life situations") and „Sonderausgaben“ ("Special expenses") ask at the top „für mich / für ‹Name›“ ("for me / for ‹name›"). B's answers are in B's record (interview?person=B, only these two catalogs). Joint catalogs stay with the user (400 or 409).

  3. Pension for B. The pension interface takes ?person=B. The pension form has a partner mode. The joint estimate counts a pension as income: taxable share minus the €102 lump sum (§ 9a S. 1 Nr. 3 EStG), nothing withheld. A pension alongside a salary of the same person is left out.

  4. Kinderzulage per child with payer. Each child can state with whose salary the allowance comes (A or B, new column schweizerKinderzulageVon). The distribution (swissAllowancePerChild) works like this:

    • A separate amount on the child takes precedence.
    • Otherwise the children of a payer share that payer's family allowances (N-Gre l. 8, in euros).
    • Children without a payer share what is assigned to no child.

    The children form and the printout use the same function. The form shows a merely computed allowance as a hint and does not store it as a fixed amount.

  5. The Lohnausweis reads family allowances from the remarks (item 15): child, education and family allowances with an amount, parts with „davon“ ("of which") not counted twice. From this a draft for N-Gre l. 8 is created for the correct person.

  6. No Grenzgänger, no estimate. With more than 60 non-return days or residence in Switzerland, the Swiss salary is exempt in Germany (Progressionsvorbehalt (progression clause)). The estimate then says "not possible" (kein-grenzgaenger) instead of fully taxing it.

  7. Documents from a photo or scan yield „prüfen“ also as a single draft (ADR-0060).

#Consequences

  • Scenario 26 („Familie Brandt“, phone width) checks:
    • B retired with the pension field;
    • the Kinderzulage with payer;
    • „Für wen?“ in the Sonderausgaben;
    • B's pension in the estimate;
    • a B with their own account who enters a change year themselves.
  • Open is the assessment comparison: for A it takes only the gross salary of the LStB and needs B's zvE (taxable income) from the client. For Grenzgänger it is therefore incomplete.
  • Also open: the Progressionsvorbehalt for non-genuine Grenzgänger.
