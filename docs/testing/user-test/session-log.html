#Roles

  • Facilitator: reads aloud, answers no questions about the app („Was würden Sie jetzt tun?“ / "What would you do now?"), steps in only after the time limit or when the person has been stuck for more than 2 minutes. Every intervention is noted.
  • Note-taker: stopwatch, notes, no comments.

#Flow

  1. Welcome (5 min) — read this text aloud:

    „Danke, dass Sie mitmachen. Wir testen die App, nicht Sie. Es gibt keine falschen Antworten. Denken Sie bitte laut: Sagen Sie, was Sie suchen, was Sie erwarten und was Sie überrascht. Alle Angaben sind ausgedacht, bitte tragen Sie nichts Eigenes ein. Sie können jederzeit abbrechen.“

    (English: "Thank you for taking part. We are testing the app, not you. There are no wrong answers. Please think aloud: say what you are looking for, what you expect and what surprises you. All details are made up; please do not enter anything of your own. You can stop at any time.") Have the participant sign the consent (notes; recording only if explicitly agreed).

  2. Tasks 1–4 (25 min) — card by card from tasks.html. Do not praise or judge between the tasks.
  3. SUS (5 min) — have the participant fill in the form from sus-questionnaire.html alone, directly after task 4, before any conversation.
  4. Debrief (5 min) — three questions:
    • „Was war am schwierigsten?“ ("What was the most difficult?")
    • „Gab es eine Stelle, an der Sie nicht wussten, wo Sie gerade sind?“ ("Was there a point where you did not know where you were?")
    • „Würden Sie Ihre echte Erklärung damit machen? Warum (nicht)?“ ("Would you do your real tax return with it? Why (not)?")
  5. Afterwards — delete the test account, file the notes under the code.

#Rules for comparable results

  • Facilitator and note-taker remain the same people across all 10 sessions.
  • Assignment to the variant alternates by appointment (1st appointment old, 2nd new, …), so that time of day and the facilitator's practice are spread out.
  • Technical faults (server gone, browser hangs) do not count as errors; the time is paused and noted.
