Architecture as of 2026-11-22

Decision · ADR-0062

Household at account level: create spouse and children once, select them per year Accepted

  • Context
  • Decision
  • Consequences
  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-10
  • Deciders: NexusHero (Product Owner), Steuereule Engineering
  • Applies to: apps/api/src/household/, apps/api/prisma/schema.prisma, packages/core/src/common/household.ts, children form, master data (linking)
  • Partially supersedes: product ADR-006 (linking per tax year) and ADR-0054 §3 (invitation code per year)
  • Ticket: #452 (grilling 10.11.2026, decisions 1–12)

#Context

Today the spouse and children are created anew for each tax year: Kind hangs off userId + steuerjahr, the link (SteuerjahrKopplung) applies for one year, a partner without an account gets a new placeholder spouse_<user>_<jahr> each year, and with an account every year needs a new invitation code. Anyone who comes back every year types name, date of birth and Steuer-ID (tax identification number) again.

The tax calculation, the printout and the Veranlagungsartenvergleich (comparison of assessment types) read exactly these year rows. Their results must not change because of the switch (AC 10, all 24 scenarios).

#Decision

#1. Expand, do not rebuild (ADR-0055, expand)

The year rows remain what the calculation reads: Kind (all fields of the Anlage Kind (child schedule)) and SteuerjahrKopplung (assessment type and Person B of the year). Next to them comes the household as the source for every new year. No calculation reads the household; that is why the results before and after the migration are identical.

#2. New models

Model Content
HouseholdPerson (HaushaltPerson) Spouse or child, once per household. Stable details (first name, last name, date of birth, Steuer-ID, for spouses the date of death) sit in an envelope, encrypted with a separate key per person in the keystore (ADR-0059, key ID household-person:<id>). leftFromYear = "no longer included from year X". Editor and time of the last change.
TaxYearMembership (HaushaltJahr) Person × tax year, with a reference to the year row (Kind.id or Person B of the link). The yearly details (Kindergeld, registered with me, KV/PV, education from 18, assessment type) stay in the year row.
PartnerLink (PartnerVerknuepfung) Spouse link without a year: Person A, Person B (own account or household person without an account), validFromYear, endedFromYear.
HouseholdMergeCandidate (HaushaltDublette) Two persons who are similar but not the same; the app asks once „Sind das dieselben?“ ("Are these the same?").

The household belongs to Person A's account. A linked partner with their own account reads and writes the shared children too (decision 7); own data (wages, Steuer-ID, master data) each person edits only themselves. Every change to a household person logs the editor and time; the form shows „geändert von …, Datum“ ("changed by …, date").

#3. Spouse

  • Once created, or once linked via the invitation code, the link applies from validFromYear for all following years, until one of them ends it with "no longer included from year X" (AC 2). No more invitation code per year.
  • Change of assessment type (joint ↔ separate, Product Owner decision of 10.11.2026): The link stays in place. The assessment type remains a per-year detail in SteuerjahrKopplung.
  • If Person A chooses "joint" in a new year, the year row is filled from the link: with an own account, Person B directly; without an account, the placeholder of the year, pre-filled from the household person.
  • A deceased spouse remains selectable in the following year (Gnadensplitting (widow's splitting), § 32a Abs. 6 Nr. 1 EStG).

#4. New tax year

Persons with a membership in the previous year are preselected and marked „aus 2025“ ("from 2025"); one click confirms and creates the year row with the stable details. Children who are older than 25 in the new year are not preselected, but remain selectable. From 18 the form asks about education; the answer is stored, but does not change the calculation in this switch (AC 10) and is marked as such.

#5. Migration (repeatable, loses nothing)

The stable fields are field-encrypted; an SQL migration cannot read them. That is why the API migrates on the first access to the household, per account, idempotently:

  1. All Kind rows and partner placeholders of the account are grouped by normalized name (upper/lower case, umlauts, whitespace) and date of birth. Equal → one person, every year row gets a membership.
  2. Similar but not equal (name distance ≤ 2 with the same date of birth, or the same name with a day/month difference in the date of birth) → two persons and one candidate.
  3. Year rows that are already assigned are skipped. No year row is changed or deleted.

The grouping is a pure function in packages/core and is unit-tested.

#6. Removing

  • "No longer included from year X" sets leftFromYear; earlier years stay unchanged (AC 5).
  • "Delete permanently" deletes the person's year rows in all years, their memberships and their key in the keystore (crypto-shredding, ADR-0059). The form warns clearly that completed years are affected too.

#7. What is not coming

  • Familienkasse (family benefits office) from the Kindergeld number (decision 9): The format is officially documented (11 characters, xxxFKxxxxxx, Merkblatt Kindergeld KG 2 of the BA, retrieved on 10.11.2026). That the first three digits identify the Familienkasse is stated in no official source, and there is no official mapping list. Following the Product Owner's direction, this helper is dropped.

#Consequences

  • Account deletion, the DSGVO (GDPR) export and the list of encrypted fields cover the new models.
  • A later contract step (ADR-0055) can remove the stable fields from Kind once all accounts are migrated and the calculation reads them from the household. That is not part of #452.
  • The regression across all 24 scenarios is the acceptance gate: same range before and after the migration.
