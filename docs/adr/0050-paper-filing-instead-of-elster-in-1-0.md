# ADR-0050 — 1.0 files on paper: form printout instead of ELSTER, paid per tax year at printout

- **Status:** Accepted (stakeholder decision 2026-10-28) — Musti review of the PR is pending
- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Deciders:** NexusHero (stakeholder): ELSTER out of 1.0, printout of the completed
  forms; payment at printout; Brevo as email provider; delivery in one PR
- **Amends** [ADR-0036](0036-definition-of-done.md) (DoD 1.0, criterion 7) and the roadmap decision
  D8 (`docs/roadmap-1.0.md`); replaces, for 1.0, the "pay at submission" from product ADR-017
- **Context tags:** product scope, compliance, payment, DSGVO

## Context

The 1.0 roadmap depended on an external pacemaker: the ELSTER (German electronic tax filing system) vendor certificate (ticket 07).
The approval is still pending, and the pricing model (product ADR-017: "pay at submission") had
no payment point without transmission — so the app could not be sold.

Legally, the paper route is open for the target group: electronic filing is mandatory only for
profit income (§ 25 Abs. 4 EStG); the app excludes that via the business gate.
Employees, pensioners, capital, rental income and Grenzgänger (cross-border commuters) CH may file on paper if the
officially prescribed form is used (§ 150 Abs. 1 AO). The BMF letter of
12.08.2022 (IV A 5 - O 1561/19/10001 :004) governs what a printout must meet for this:

- **Official internet forms** (provided on the tax administration's websites and
  printed out) and **non-official forms** (following the pattern of an approved print template,
  in particular generated by software) are both permitted.
- Non-official forms take over layout, dimensions, wording, code numbers, line numbers
  and page order completely; green print becomes greyscale (15 % coverage); no barcodes; the
  form key without "NET"; the vendor name is in the footer.
- Entries: DIN A4 portrait, at least 10 pt, sans-serif, decimal comma without thousands separator,
  empty fields stay empty, minus before the amount; all pages of a form are submitted.

## Decision

1. **1.0 files on paper.** Per tax year the app produces the completed forms
   (main form ESt 1 A and the Anlagen (schedules) in use) as a print-ready PDF. The user checks,
   signs and submits. ELSTER transmission is **no longer a 1.0 criterion**; it comes
   as a 1.x extension as soon as the certificate is available (ticket 07 continues).

2. **Templates are checked in per tax year, not fetched at runtime.** The
   tax administration's form management system delivers its PDFs only session-bound via
   a form engine; automated retrieval is neither stable nor cleared. The blank
   Vordrucke (official forms) of a year are obtained once and stored under `apps/api/assets/vordrucke/<jahr>/`;
   every field position is tested against exactly this template. Until the templates are there,
   the app offers no printout — no improvised layout that looks like an official form.
   **Open, to be clarified before the first sale:** whether a machine-filled portal PDF (key
   with "NET") counts as an official internet form or must be produced as a non-official form with
   grey screening and vendor details (tax advisor/Finanzamt (tax office)).

3. **Payment happens at printout, once per tax year.** Capturing, reviewing and the range stay
   free of charge. Unlocking runs via Stripe Checkout (hosted page, no card data on
   our server, invoice via Stripe). **Only the signature-verified webhook unlocks** — the
   browser redirect proves nothing. Before payment the user agrees in checkout to the
   immediate start and confirms the expiry of the right of withdrawal (§ 356 Abs. 5 BGB). Without
   Stripe configuration, unlocking is honestly "not available".

4. **Email via Brevo (EU).** The `EmailSender` seam (ADR-0012 §6) gets the production
   provider; production without a provider does not boot (`EMAIL_DELIVERY=log` as a deliberate choice
   for the local prod stack). Closes #83.

5. **Legal pages in the product.** Impressum (legal notice), privacy policy, terms and conditions with withdrawal notice and
   a "no tax advice" notice exist as code, written against the real behavior;
   provider details come from the build environment. The texts are a **draft** and will be legally
   reviewed before the first sale.

## Consequences

- 1.0 no longer has an external authority pacemaker; what still blocks it is on us
  (infra track #292, templates, legal review, DSGVO (GDPR) audit, run-through on a real URL).
- Each tax year creates maintenance effort: obtain new templates, update field positions. That
  is the price for being able to sell without a certificate.
- The printout itself is the next slice (ticket 11, „Abgabe-bereit-Paket" ("ready-to-file package")); the
  unlock (`FreischaltungService.status`) is its gate and is already built.
- The translation "pay at submission" → "pay at printout" changes the moment for the user,
  not the principle: payment happens only once the result is there.

## Implementation of the templates (2026-10-28, same PR)

- The 2025 templates came from the stakeholder as a ZIP from the form management system:
  official internet forms (form key `…NET`, barcodes, already grey-screened),
  **without form fields**. They are stored unchanged under `apps/api/assets/vordrucke/2025/`,
  pinned via SHA-256 (`apps/api/src/printout/vordrucke-2025.json`). Anlage R and V were delivered later.
- Field positions are not typed in but measured from the templates with `scripts/vordrucke/vermessen.mjs`:
  the anchor is a printed word (label, code number, line number), the target is the
  white input box or frame that the form itself draws. The result
  (`felder-2025.json`) carries the SHA-256 of each template; a swap without re-measurement turns red.
- The template pages are taken over unchanged, only values are printed on them. This keeps
  the printout as close as possible to the official internet form; the legal classification
  (point 2, "open") is unaffected by this and still to be clarified before the first sale.
- The main form needs details the app did not capture before (address,
  date of birth, religion, marital status, IBAN, Finanzamt): a separate, encrypted model
  `Stammdaten` (master data, REQ-030); religion only with consent under Art. 9 DSGVO.
- Deliberately empty or locked: Lohnsteuer Kz 140 (the app only knows whole euros, the eDaten are available);
  married users, until assessment type and Person B are captured.
- **No incomplete tax return:** if someone has captured income whose Anlage is not printable yet
  (V, KAP, N-Gre, company pension → R-AV/bAV, foreign pension → R-AUS), the printout is locked
  with names. The pre-check (`GET …/ausdruck/pruefung`) shows this **before** payment, and the
  checkout sits at the printout (`POST …/ausdruck/checkout`) and refuses as long as something
  is missing — nobody pays for a printout that cannot be delivered.

## Amendment — 2026-11-05: verified field positions and editable variant

**Safeguard against changed forms.** The positions still come from anchors
(`felder-<jahr>.spec.json` → `vermessen.mjs`). New: the CI job „Vordrucke“ checks that
`felder-<jahr>.json` matches what templates and spec produce (including the checksum of each template),
and that the position report `felder-<jahr>.lage.txt` — line, label and column header per field —
is unchanged. A changed report requires a human review (procedure:
`docs/process/official-forms-at-year-change.md`). The official FFWP format is encrypted and has no
specification; a custom parser was rejected.

**Editable variant (AcroForm).** `GET …/ausdruck?bearbeitbar=true` returns the same values as
named AcroForm text fields over the same boxes (`anlKap.kz280`, instances `anlKind.vorname#2`),
the same locks and the same payment. The printout itself stays fixed page content: a form
flattened with pdf-lib leaves orphaned object references that PDF readers report as broken;
the direct text is pixel-identical to the previous printout. The app points out that changes
in the editable file diverge from the details in the app.

- 2026-11-05: stakeholder decision (NexusHero), FFWP rejected, AcroForm as an addition.
