Decision · ADR-0070

Owl: savings plan, outlook, Bescheid check and „Unsere Situation“ ("Our situation") Accepted

  • Context
  • Decision
  • Consequences
  • Rejected
  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-12
  • Deciders: NexusHero (Product Owner), Steuereule Engineering
  • Applies to: Ticket #501; packages/core/src/interview/{advisor-tasks,advisor-situation}.ts, apps/api/src/ki/{advisor-tasks,advisor-stance,advisor-prompt,advisor-tools,advisor-tool-round}.ts, apps/api/src/advisor/, apps/mobile-web/src/screens/advisor/chat/Situation.tsx
  • Supplements: ADR-0067 (notes), ADR-0069 (advisor stance)

#Context

The idea of the product is an owl that advises and does not just fill in. With ADR-0069 it has the stance and the knowledge. But it still waited for questions, saw only a brief summary of the one tax year of the case, and its work ended after filing.

#Decision

  1. Savings plan (quick start). The owl goes through the whole case against the catalog of savings levers. Which levers are open is determined by the app without a model (unusedSavingsLevers): what is empty in the case overview ("open") and for what there is no field ("ask"). The answer has a fixed structure: take now (by amount), a detail is missing for this, current and next year. No amount without a tool.
  2. An outlook belongs to every consultation (stance B11): what the user should change in the current and the next year.
  3. Bescheid check (quick start). The owl receives the comparison the app already calculates (deviations per field, status, remaining days of the appeal deadline), and no other numbers. If a deviation is to the user's disadvantage and the deadline is running, it drafts the appeal in full. Without a recorded Bescheid (tax assessment notice) it asks for the values and invents nothing.
  4. Every quick start begins with a plan, so that the user sees what the owl is doing.
  5. „Unsere Situation“ is the starting knowledge of every conversation. Two parts, both without names:
    • a profile the app builds from the recorded details (assessment type, occupation, children with age at year end, wage rounded, commute, Grenzgänger (cross-border commuter), rental income, capital) that comes first in the case-specific prompt;
    • a text by the user in their own words (at most 1,500 characters), one per user, valid across all tax years, stored encrypted. Names and identifiers are removed on saving.
  6. The text belongs to the user. The owl does not write it. If it learns something lasting, it suggests a sentence with suggest_situation (at most three per answer, without personal data); it is only stored after a tap. This is deliberately stricter than for the project notes (ADR-0067 § 3), because this text has an effect over years.
  7. The advisor stance is in every model call. A test checks this for every provider, with and without a skill, for every quick start and for the second model.

#Consequences

  • Data export and account deletion cover the text of the situation.
  • The savings plan is as good as the case overview: what the app does not record (tradespeople, work equipment, medical costs), the owl can only ask about.
  • If no model answers, there is neither a savings plan nor a Bescheid check, only the fixed rules.
  • Not tested against a real model.

#Rejected

  • Savings plan automatically on every opening: a paid model call without any action by the user. Instead it is the first quick start on the empty chat.
  • The owl maintains the situation itself: see 6.
  • Letting the model remember the levers: then completeness would depend on the model's memory.
