Decision · ADR-0083

Lohnsteuerbescheinigung without readable social insurance Accepted

  • Context
  • Decision
  • Consequences
  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-13
  • Deciders: NexusHero (Product Owner), Steuereule Engineering
  • Applies to: apps/api/src/lstb/lstb.service.ts, apps/api/src/lstb-scan/, apps/mobile-web/src/screens/lstb/scan/
  • Builds on: ADR-0008 (applying values), adding a further certificate (scan robustness)

#Context

In the 2024 real case there was a second Lohnsteuerbescheinigung (wage tax certificate) from the same employer (payment after leaving, tax class 6). Gross salary and wage tax were readable, the social insurance lines in the scan were not. „Werte übernehmen“ ("Apply values") required all three values, so the salary and tax of this certificate were missing entirely.

#Decision

  1. The social insurance may be missing. Gross salary and wage tax are applied. If the social insurance stays empty, it counts as open.
  2. For a further certificate the salary and tax are added to the existing total. The social insurance total stays in place with its provenance.
  3. A further certificate counts as the same certificate only if all values read are the same.
  4. Form: an empty social insurance field does not block „Werte übernehmen“. A hint says what then happens and that the user can also enter the amount themselves. The plausibility check treats the missing social insurance as 0.

#Consequences

  • 2024 real case: salary and wage tax of both certificates appear together in the Lohnsteuerbescheinigung, as with the tax advisor.
  • The owl's drafts for a scanned certificate stay as they are; values are applied via the form.
