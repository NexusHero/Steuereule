#Context

In 1.0 capital income becomes a full income type (product ADR-032): German depots (Abgeltungsteuer (flat-rate withholding tax), Sparerpauschbetrag (saver's lump-sum allowance), Günstigerprüfung (most-favorable check) § 32d Abs. 6) plus foreign brokers (IBKR, Revolut — no tax withheld → Anlage KAP (capital income form) obligation; exactly the young target group). Crypto stays 2.0 (private sale transaction, holding periods, its own sourcing logic — an honest gate). The interview answer kap-depot (Deutscher Broker / Ausländischer Broker / Krypto) already exists in the segment 2 graph (interview.ts KAP_DEPOT_VALUES) and gatedEinkunftsarten already gates Kapital for the crypto answer — what is missing is the item/Anlage KAP build-out: the guided data interface for the Jahressteuerbescheinigung (annual bank tax certificate), the depot classification, and the § 32d Günstigerprüfung as a bounded comparison following the ADR-0034 pattern.

Naming discipline (ADR-0034 §1): this ticket carries the name "Günstigerprüfung" (§ 32d Abs. 6 EStG) — the Veranlagungsartenvergleich (comparison of assessment types, 10.2) is not called that, and vice versa. Two different comparisons, two names, never again one.

#Decision

A pure, UI-free module packages/core/src/capital.ts, a second one packages/core/src/most-favourable-check.ts (ADR-0004, no I/O), plus a document type extension, plus the usual API/screen build-out:

  1. Depot classification (ordneKapDepotEin) — the kap-depot answer is mapped to what it MEANS for the tax return: deutscher-broker (Abgeltungsteuer withheld — the Günstigerprüfung is the applicable question here), auslaendischer-broker (no German tax withheld → mandatory assessment Anlage KAP, taxation at the standard tariff — the KAP obligation is named honestly, a flat-rate withholding is never invented), krypto (gated, ADR-032 §2), unbekannt (never guessed). The classification is a pure derivation from the ONE source (interview answer) — no denormalized column that can drift.

  2. Guided data interface (KapitalDaten) — one captured fact: the capital income (income from capital assets, whole euros), erfasst/offen/unklar (the same honesty state machine as lstb.ts/rente.ts). Serialization in ONE place (ADR-0033 one-rule instinct). Folding: kapitalAlsKernposten → Posten of income type Kapital with Quelle: { art: 'eingabe' } (the guided form is a transcription; the document/AI-suggestion provenance is already carried by the Quelle contract — it is the assignment slice, a named follow-up, not a half feature here); kapitalStand folds via berechne and passes gegated through unchanged — a crypto investor reads open with a named gate.

  3. guenstigerpruefung.ts — § 32d Abs. 6 EStG as a bounded comparison (ADR-0034 pattern):

    • abgeltung: 25 % on (capital income − Sparerpauschbetrag § 20 Abs. 9: €1,000 single / €2,000 joint) + Soli (§ 4 SolZG) + KiSt with the § 32d Abs. 1 Satz 4 reduction (the Abgeltungsteuer is reduced by 25 % of the church tax attributable to it). All rates cited, no invented constant.
    • persoenlich: the tariff tax on the capital income share = § 32a tariff on (remaining zvE + capital income) − tariff on remaining zvE, plus the Soli difference (the exemption limit makes the Soli non-linear — a difference, never a surcharge on the difference) and the KiSt; Zusammenveranlagung (joint assessment) = splitting (§ 32a Abs. 5) on the combined basis.
    • The result is the ADR-0034 union: aussage eindeutig (with empfehlung) / zu-knapp / unbestimmt — empfehlung exists ONLY on eindeutig, the compiler refuses the shortcut. unschaerfe is the ONE bound definition from the Veranlagungsartenvergleich (progressionsvorbehaltSchranke, exported for this): the Progressionsvorbehalt (§ 32b) is the only excluded factor that can tip the result. eindeutig only if the bound is determined AND |delta| exceeds it. Level 3 stays open: uebrigesZvE is an income basis (not a real zvE), and herkunft honestly carries that along.

  4. Steuerbescheinigung as a document type — the Belegart union (ADR-0040) grows by one value (the bank's annual tax certificate), no rename, no change in semantics of existing values.

  5. API (Kapital module) + screen — GET/PUT /v1/steuerjahre/{jahr}/kapital[/ {feld}], one row per (userId, steuerjahr), kapitalertraege field-encrypted at rest (ADR-0008). GET .../kapital/guenstigerpruefung computes ONLY via @steuereule/core and names the honest gates (nicht-anwendbar): foreign broker (keine-abgeltung-einbehalten), crypto (krypto-gegated), unanswered kap-depot (kap-depot-nicht-beantwortet), missing capital income (keine-kapitalertraege) — never an invented comparison. The screen shows the depot classification, the field, the item with provenance and the Günstigerprüfung with aussage first.

#Consequences

  • M1 limits, named once: uebrigesZvE of the Günstigerprüfung is the LStB income basis of the same tax year (as the Veranlagungsartenvergleich 10.2 uses it) — pension/ rental income/CH are not yet folded in (level 3 open, carries herkunft). Zusammenveranlagung is computed by the core, the API serves M1 single (partner figures are never invented — a named follow-up). KiSt is a query input (M1 does not collect it yet, default 0).
  • Honest limit, named once: loss offsetting, crediting of foreign Quellensteuer (withholding tax) (§ 32d Abs. 5), § 32d Abs. 2 cases (shareholdings) and the KiSt cap are NOT here — named follow-ups, no silent approximation.
  • Crypto stays 2.0: the gate is the honest statement (screen + gatedEinkunftsarten), not a half feature (ADR-032 §2).
  • Security posture: kapitalertraege is field-encrypted at rest (ADR-0008) — actual capital assets are among the most sensitive facts, joinable to the Steuer-ID; the depot classification is NOT its own column, but the (likewise encrypted) interview answer kap-depot; WRITE/READ audited (REQ-004). No performance axis.
  • Register: REQ-025; the neighboring tickets occupy 022/023/024 — check for collisions on merge (10.6 takes 026).

#Alternatives considered

  • Abgeltungsteuer without Sparerpauschbetrag. Rejected: § 20 Abs. 9 EStG is citable and changes the assessment basis considerably — leaving it out would be a silent falsification, not a simplification.
  • KiSt as an uncertainty bound instead of folded in. Rejected: the § 32d Abs. 1 Satz 4 reduction is citable; once folded in, the uncertainty remains exclusively the Progressionsvorbehalt — the same shape as the Veranlagungsartenvergleich (ADR-0043), one bound definition for both.
  • Depot type as its own Prisma column. Rejected: a denormalized copy of the interview answer can drift (ADR-0033 defect class); the derivation from the one source is pure and testable.
  • "Make up" a Günstigerprüfung for foreign brokers as well. Rejected: there, Abgeltungsteuer was never withheld, the comparison is moot — the honest nicht-anwendbar answer is itself the naming of the KAP obligation (ADR-032).
