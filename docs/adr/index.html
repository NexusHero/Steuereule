Architecture decisions for the engineering build, in lightweight MADR (Context → Decision → Consequences [→ Alternatives]). Numbering is 4-digit, sequential and immutable; a decision that replaces another supersedes it (never rewrite history). Governed by ultimate-dev-process §1.2.

The ADRs justify the architecture; docs/index.html shows it (arc42: context, building blocks, runtime, deployment, concepts, risks). The toolchain and the boot-ordering invariant are detailed in docs/arc42/README.html.
