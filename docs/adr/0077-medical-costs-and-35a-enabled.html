Decision · ADR-0077

Medical costs (§ 33) and household-related expenses (§ 35a) enabled Accepted

  • Context
  • Decision
  • Consequences
  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-13
  • Deciders: NexusHero (Product Owner), Steuereule Engineering
  • Applies to: apps/api/src/config/features.ts, apps/mobile-web/src/config/features.ts, apps/api/src/printout/{printout-values,printout.service,form-renderer}.ts, apps/api/src/printout/felder-2025.{spec.json,json}
  • Builds on: ADR-0032 (switch HOUSEHOLD_AND_HEALTH_BOOSTERS), ADR-0050 (measuring the printout)

#Context

The questions about medical costs and household-related expenses and their calculation (reasonable burden, 20 % tax reduction) were built but switched off: at the time there was no reliable total amount of income, and no Vordruck (official form) could print the values. The estimate now computes the total amount of income itself. In the real case the chiropractor and the tradesman labor share, which the tax advisor had claimed, were therefore missing.

#Decision

  1. The switch is on (API and app). The tile „Haushalt & Handwerker“ ("Household & tradesmen") is at the front for everyone, „Gesundheit“ ("Health") at the front for pensioners, parents and single parents, otherwise under „Weitere Themen“ ("More topics").
  2. The printout fills the Anlage Außergewöhnliche Belastungen l. 23–25 (Kz 302, 303 = 0, because the interview asks for the costs after reimbursements) and the Anlage Haushaltsnahe Aufwendungen l. 4 (Kz 202), l. 5 (Kz 212) and l. 6/9 (Kz 214). Both Vordrucke are newly measured and come after the Sonderausgaben (special expenses).
  3. If the switch is off, the printout prints none of this and the interview stores none of it.

#Consequences

  • Medical costs are one total; doctor, dentist, glasses are not kept separately.
  • For tradesmen only the labor share is known; the user adds the invoice amount in l. 6 in the printout.
