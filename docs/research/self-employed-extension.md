# Self-employed: extension 2022–2026 — sources

As of: 20.11.2026. Supplements `self-employed-tax-rules.md` (what was already checked there is not repeated here). "Checked" means: read in the statutory text on gesetze-im-internet.de, in the BMF letter, in the BMF's official Handausgabe (annotated edition), on minijob-zentrale.de or in the taxonomy package of esteuer.de itself. gesetze-im-internet.de only shows the current version; earlier values are therefore marked as checked only where a transitional provision, a BMF letter or a handbook of the respective years names them.

## 1. Private car use, trips between home and business

Provisions: [§ 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 4 Satz 2, 3 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__6.html), [§ 52 Abs. 12 Sätze 5, 6 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__52.html), [§ 4 Abs. 5 Satz 1 Nr. 6 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__4.html).

- 1 % per month of the domestic gross list price at first registration plus optional extras including VAT, only if the vehicle is used for business **more than 50 %** (§ 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 4 Satz 2). Checked.
- Logbook instead of the flat rate: actual costs of the private trips with documents and a proper logbook (Satz 3); electric/hybrid there with a quarter/half of the acquisition costs. Checked.
- List-price fraction per acquisition date (Satz 2 Nr. 2–5; rounding down to full €100 happens after applying the fraction, BMF 05.11.2021, BStBl I S. 2205, Rn. 12):

  The fraction also applies to used vehicles (Rn. 11). CO₂ and range from the certificate of conformity (end of Satz 2). Checked.
- Cost cap: the usage value and the non-deductible costs of trips between home and business together at most the total costs of the vehicle, permitted per vehicle; total costs including AfA (depreciation), for electric/hybrid with the reduced AfA base. BMF 18.11.2009, BStBl I S. 1326, Rn. 18 (amended by BMF 15.11.2012, BStBl I S. 1099), in [EStH 2024 Anhang 16 III](https://esth.bundesfinanzministerium.de/esth/2024/C-Anhaenge/Anhang-16/III/anhang-16-III.html); BMF 05.11.2021 Rn. 13. Checked. No cap at 50 % of the total costs (H note there, BFH 15.05.2018, BStBl II S. 712). Spread a special leasing payment over the periods it relates to (BFH 17.05.2022, BStBl II S. 829). Checked. I did not find a newer income-tax letter that replaces 18.11.2009; BMF 21.07.2026 (IV C 6 - S 2177/00016/042/056) only changes Rn. 19, 20 of the letter of 05.11.2021 (charging power, flat-rate electricity price from financial years after 31.12.2025). Checked.
- Proof of > 50 %: Rn. 4–7 of BMF 18.11.2009 (e.g. records over three months; presumption for typical travel activity only for the vehicle with the highest annual mileage; trips between home and business count as business). Checked (EStH 2024 Anhang 16 III).
- The business owner's trips between home and business with the business car (§ 4 Abs. 5 Satz 1 Nr. 6 Satz 3 EStG): non-deductible is the positive difference between **0.03 % × list price × distance km × months** and the Entfernungspauschale (distance allowance) (§ 9 Abs. 1 Satz 3 Nr. 4 Satz 2–6, rates: see `self-employed-tax-rules.md`); trips home for double housekeeping 0.002 % per km. With a logbook or individual valuation under § 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 4 Satz 1 or 3, the actual expenses. Checked. The list-price fraction for electric/hybrid also applies here (BMF 05.11.2021 Rn. 12). Checked. Worked example with 0.03 % under the cost cap: BMF 18.11.2009 Rn. 18 example. Checked.
- Umsatzsteuer (VAT) on private use (unentgeltliche Wertabgabe, free supply, § 3 Abs. 9a Nr. 1, § 10 Abs. 4 Satz 1 Nr. 2 UStG), [Abschnitt 15.23 Abs. 5 UStAE (UStH 2023)](https://usth.bundesfinanzministerium.de/usth/2023/A-Umsatzsteuergesetz/IV-Steuer-und-Vorsteuer/Paragraf-15/ae-15-23.html): under the 1 % rule the income-tax value may be taken as the starting point; the electric/hybrid special rules (Satz 2 Nr. 1–5) do **not** apply for VAT (so the full list price); for costs not burdened with input tax a flat **20 % deduction**; the rest is the net value, 19 % on top. Gives taxable amount = 80 % of the 1 % value. Checked. That trips between home and business count as business for VAT (no free supply): not checked against the primary text.

## 6. Sale and discontinuation of a business

- [§ 16 Abs. 4 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__16.html): on application an allowance of **€45,000** if the person has completed their 55th year of life or is permanently unable to work under social security law; once in a lifetime; reduced by the amount by which the gain exceeds **€136,000** (so zero at €181,000). Checked against the current text; whether the same in 2022–2026: no transitional rule found, not checked further.
- [§ 34 Abs. 1 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__34.html) one-fifth rule: tax = 5 × (ESt(taxable income excluding extraordinary income + 1/5 extraordinary) − ESt(taxable income excluding extraordinary)). Checked.
- § 34 Abs. 3 EStG: on application, portion up to **€5 million**, age 55 or inability to work; **56 %** of the average tax rate on the whole taxable income including Progressionsvorbehalt (progression clause), **at least 14 %**; once in a lifetime, with several gains in one VZ only for one of them. Checked against the current text. The minimum rate is in the statute as a fixed value „14 Prozent“, not as a reference to the entry tax rate; since when: not checked.
- § 34a Abs. 1 Satz 1 last half-sentence: no § 34a to the extent that § 16 Abs. 4 or § 34 Abs. 3 is used. Checked.

## 8. E-invoice
