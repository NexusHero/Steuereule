#6. Sale and discontinuation of a business

  • § 16 Abs. 4 EStG: on application an allowance of €45,000 if the person has completed their 55th year of life or is permanently unable to work under social security law; once in a lifetime; reduced by the amount by which the gain exceeds €136,000 (so zero at €181,000). Checked against the current text; whether the same in 2022–2026: no transitional rule found, not checked further.
  • § 34 Abs. 1 EStG one-fifth rule: tax = 5 × (ESt(taxable income excluding extraordinary income + 1/5 extraordinary) − ESt(taxable income excluding extraordinary)). Checked.
  • § 34 Abs. 3 EStG: on application, portion up to €5 million, age 55 or inability to work; 56 % of the average tax rate on the whole taxable income including Progressionsvorbehalt (progression clause), at least 14 %; once in a lifetime, with several gains in one VZ only for one of them. Checked against the current text. The minimum rate is in the statute as a fixed value „14 Prozent“, not as a reference to the entry tax rate; since when: not checked.
  • § 34a Abs. 1 Satz 1 last half-sentence: no § 34a to the extent that § 16 Abs. 4 or § 34 Abs. 3 is used. Checked.

#8. E-invoice

  • § 14 Abs. 1 UStG from 1.1.2025: e-invoice = structured electronic format that is issued, transmitted and received and can be processed electronically; format under EN 16931 (Satz 6 Nr. 1) or agreed, if it can be extracted losslessly into an EN 16931-compliant format (Nr. 2). Obligation to use e-invoices between domestic businesses (Abs. 2 Satz 2 Nr. 1). Checked.
  • Receipt: all domestic businesses must be able to receive e-invoices, including Kleinunternehmer; an email inbox is sufficient, no right to a substitute invoice (Abschnitt 14.1 Abs. 5 UStAE as amended by BMF 15.10.2025, III C 2 - S 7287-a/00019/007/243, which amends BMF 15.10.2024, BStBl I S. 1320). Checked.

#13. Statutory review of 20.11.2026

The rules in packages/core/src/domains/business and the provision expenses were checked against the official texts: gesetze-im-internet.de, BGBl, BMF letters, the 2026 Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung form and the Minijob-Zentrale.

Fixed:

  • § 34a Abs. 2 Satz 3 EStG (from VZ 2024): Withdrawals are deemed to be primarily payment of the retention tax and its Soli. The app sets this itself, as a fixed point, because the tax depends on the exempted amount. The input field now only applies to tax from earlier years.
  • § 34a Abs. 6 Nr. 1 EStG: A sale or discontinuation subjects the entire amount subject to subsequent taxation to subsequent taxation at 25 %. Previously § 34a was dropped entirely in the year of the sale. Abs. 1, however, only excludes the preferential sale gain („soweit“).
  • BMF 05.11.2021 Rn. 13: In the cost cap for electric and hybrid cars, the AfA counts in the total costs with the same fraction as the list price.
  • Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung 2026, Kz 86: It counts all employees, including temporary workers and minijobbers (note no. 11).
  • § 18 Abs. 2 Satz 3 UStG: A founder who expects a refund is not exempt.
  • Citations corrected: § 34 Abs. 3 Satz 5 instead of Satz 4; § 19 Abs. 1 Satz 4 UStG only applies until 2024, from 2025 § 15 Abs. 2 Satz 1 Nr. 1 excludes the input tax deduction.
