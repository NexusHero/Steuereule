Research: cross-border commuters

Swiss Kinderzulage (FamZG) for a Grenzgänger: deduction in Anlage N-Gre?

  • Short answer
  • 1. Legal basis of the tax exemption
  • 2. Wording of the guide to Anlage N-Gre
  • 3. Progressionsvorbehalt and Günstigerprüfung
  • 4. Result

Research as of: 22.11.2026. Primary sources: ELSTER guide (tax administration), gesetze-im-internet.de, bundesfinanzhof.de. Case: Grenzgänger (cross-border commuter) in BW (Art. 15a DBA CH), Kinderzulage (Swiss child allowance) 2024 400 CHF (Nov–Dec), 2025 2.580 CHF (215 CHF/month), child in DE, differential child benefit.

#Short answer

Yes, the deduction is correct. The Kinderzulage is tax-free in Germany, is deducted from the Swiss gross salary (2024 line 7, 2025 line 8 of Anlage N-Gre) and is not subject to the Progressionsvorbehalt (progression proviso). In the Günstigerprüfung (most-favorable-treatment check, § 31 EStG), however, it is counted like Kindergeld, at most up to the amount of German Kindergeld. One consequence that is easy to overlook: the 4,5 % Quellensteuer (withholding tax) attributable to the allowance portion cannot be credited.

Certainty: high for the result (deduction, no Progressionsvorbehalt). Medium for the exact legal basis, because no source names a number in § 3 EStG.

#1. Legal basis of the tax exemption

  • § 3 Nr. 24 EStG does not fit its wording. It only exempts „Leistungen, die auf Grund des Bundeskindergeldgesetzes gewährt werden“ (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__3.html). Benefits under the Swiss FamZG do not fall under it.
  • § 3 Nr. 11 EStG does not fit. It excludes child supplements based on pay statutes, collective agreements „oder ähnlicher Vorschriften“ (§ 3 Nr. 11 Satz 2, same URL).
  • The tax administration relies on comparability under § 65 EStG. The guide to Anlage N-Gre names no number in § 3. It says a foreign benefit comparable to Kindergeld under § 65 EStG is „insgesamt steuerfrei“ (guide 2024 and 2025: https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2024, https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2025).
    • Under § 65 Satz 1 Nr. 1 EStG such foreign benefits exclude Kindergeld. Under Satz 2 they are treated as equivalent to Kindergeld „soweit es für die Anwendung von Vorschriften dieses Gesetzes auf den Erhalt von Kindergeld ankommt“ (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__65.html).
    • Kindergeld is a tax rebate (§ 31 Satz 3 EStG, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__31.html) and therefore not income. The equivalent foreign benefit therefore belongs to the family benefits equalization and not to taxable wages.
    • This doctrinal derivation is my own conclusion. No primary source phrases it this way.
  • Which benefits are meant: According to the guide, comparable to Kindergeld are „nur die Familienzulagen nach Artikel 3 Absatz 1“ FamZG or under the cantonal implementing acts. These are child allowances up to age 16 (at least 200 CHF) and education allowances from 16 to 25 (at least 250 CHF). Higher cantonal allowances are also tax-free, even above German Kindergeld. Other cantonal family benefits depend on the decision of the Familienkasse. The guide refers to the BZSt letter of 22.08.2024, BStBl I S. 1143 (guide, URLs above).
  • BFH case law:
    • BFH, ruling of 28.04.2020, VI R 24/17 (https://www.bundesfinanzhof.de/de/entscheidung/entscheidungen-online/detail/pdf/STRE202050199?type=1646225765): a tax advisor acts with gross negligence (§ 173 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 AO) if they do not answer the Anlage N-Gre question about „steuerfreien Kinderzulagen“. The senate assumes that Swiss Kinderzulagen included in the gross salary are tax-free and had to be deducted (paras. 13, 23). It names no provision for this.
    • BFH, ruling of 09.07.2026, III R 37/23 (https://www.bundesfinanzhof.de/de/entscheidung/entscheidungen-online/detail/pdf/STRE202610191?type=1646225765): for differential Kindergeld, the Swiss Kinderzulage is to be credited at its gross amount. The ruling concerns Kindergeld, not income tax.
  • Not verified:
    • I could not retrieve the text of the BZSt letter of 22.08.2024 (BStBl I 2024 S. 1143).
    • I did not find an OFD directive on the legal basis in primary text.
    • I know of an OFD Karlsruhe directive of 20.03.2020 (S 130.1/647 – St 217) on crediting Quellensteuer only from an online petition (https://www.openpetition.de/petition/online/beibehaltung-des-steuerfreien-kindergeldes-fuer-ch-grenzgaenger). That is not primary evidence.
    • I did not find a note H 3.24 EStH/LStH on foreign benefits. The site esth.bundesfinanzministerium.de returned HTTP 503.

#2. Wording of the guide to Anlage N-Gre

Reference: guide to Anlage N-Gre, section „Kürzungen“ (2024: lines 6 to 13, 2025: lines 7 to 14). The benefits are described there as those „die in Deutschland steuerbefreit sind“.

  • 2024: „In Zeile 7 sind Kinder- und Ausbildungszulagen auszuweisen.“ (https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2024)
  • 2025: „In Zeile 8 sind Kinder- und Ausbildungszulagen auszuweisen.“ (https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2025)
  • In both years this is followed by the sentence giving the reason that the comparable benefit is „insgesamt steuerfrei“ (§ 65 EStG).

On evidence for special benefits the guide says: the rejection of differential Kindergeld alone is not enough. It must be shown how the Familienkasse treats the specific cantonal allowance.

For the statutory Kinderzulage under Art. 3 Abs. 1 FamZG this individual evidence is not needed. It is expressly named as comparable. According to the form and the guide, the monthly salary statements must nevertheless be attached (cf. also BFH VI R 24/17, para. 24).

#3. Progressionsvorbehalt and Günstigerprüfung

No Progressionsvorbehalt.

  • § 32b Abs. 1 EStG lists the benefits exhaustively. Kindergeld and comparable benefits are not named there (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__32b.html).
  • § 32b Abs. 1 Satz 1 Nr. 3 (DBA-exempt income) does not apply. The wages are taxable in Germany under Art. 15a DBA CH, and the allowance is not a DBA exemption.
  • The guide distinguishes accordingly:
    • For ALV benefits, daily allowances, EOG benefits and IV daily allowances it additionally requires an entry in line 36 ESt 1 A (Progressionsvorbehalt).
    • For the child and education allowance it does not require this (guide 2025, URL above).

Günstigerprüfung (§ 31 EStG):

  • § 31 Satz 6 EStG: Satz 4 (adding the Kindergeld entitlement) „gilt entsprechend für mit dem Kindergeld vergleichbare Leistungen nach § 65“ (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__31.html).
  • § 31 Satz 7 EStG: an entitlement under foreign law is disregarded to the extent that it „das inländische Kindergeld übersteigt“.
  • § 2 Abs. 6 Satz 3 EStG: if the child allowances (Kinderfreibeträge) are deducted, the Kindergeld entitlement is added to the tariff tax (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__2.html).
  • For the case this means: the Swiss allowance and German differential Kindergeld together are added at most in the amount of German Kindergeld. For 2025 that is 255 € per month, for 2024 250 € per month. I have not checked these amounts against the statute here; they are in § 66 EStG.
  • For differential Kindergeld the allowance is to be counted at its gross amount (BFH III R 37/23).

Quellensteuer: According to the 2025 guide (line 25), the 4,5 % Quellensteuer is only credited „soweit die Einkünfte in der Veranlagung zur Einkommensteuer erfasst wurden“. The tax on the deducted allowance portion is therefore not creditable.

  • 2025: 4,5 % × 2.580 CHF = 116,10 CHF
  • 2024: 4,5 % × 400 CHF = 18 CHF

This consequence is derived from the guide. The underlying OFD directive is not verified (see above).

#4. Result

The deduction from taxable wages is correct.

  • In 2024, 400 CHF are deducted in line 7; in 2025, 2.580 CHF are deducted in line 8. Both amounts are converted at the rate the guide prescribes.
  • There is no entry in line 36 ESt 1 A.
  • In the Günstigerprüfung the allowance is counted like Kindergeld, at most up to German Kindergeld.
  • The Quellensteuer credit is to be reduced by the allowance portion.

Certainty: high. The result follows from the express wording of the guide (form of the BW tax administration) and from BFH VI R 24/17, which presupposes the tax exemption. The exact provision remains open; it does not change the result.

What would change the result:

  1. The payment is not a family allowance under Art. 3 Abs. 1 FamZG but a voluntary employer child allowance or an unnamed cantonal special benefit. Then it is taxable wages, or evidence from the Familienkasse is needed.
  2. The allowance is not included in item 8 of the Lohnausweis (Swiss salary certificate), for example because the family compensation fund pays directly. Then no deduction is needed, since otherwise it would be reduced twice.
  3. The child does not meet the age requirements, or the allowance concerns another child or another parent.
  4. The tax year falls in an assessment other than 2024/2025. Then the line number may change, and the wording must be checked anew each year.
  5. A change by statute or administration. The 2024 BZSt letter is unread.
