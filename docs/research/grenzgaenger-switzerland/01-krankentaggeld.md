# Swiss collective Krankentaggeld (paid out via the employer) – Grenzgänger BW, VZ 2024

Research as of: 22.11.2026. Markers: **[verified]** = primary source read directly; **[not verified]** = known only via a secondary source/citation reference, primary text not retrievable.

## Facts (short)
Lohnausweis (Swiss salary certificate) item 8 gross salary 102.736 CHF, remark „KK-Taggeld Verrechnung: 20843 CHF“. Collective Krankentaggeld (sickness daily allowance, KTG) insurance (VVG/KVG); the insurer pays the employer, the employer passes it on with the salary (included in items 1/8). The employer bears 100 % of the premiums; these are added as wages in Anlage N-Gre line 18 (2024: line 17, see below).

## 1. Wages or tax-free?

**Statute [verified]:** § 3 Nr. 1 Buchst. a EStG exempts „Leistungen aus einer Krankenversicherung, aus einer Pflegeversicherung …“; § 3 Nr. 2 Buchst. e EStG extends this to „vergleichbare Leistungen ausländischer Rechtsträger“ (EU/EEA/Switzerland).
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__3.html

**Tax administration – official guide (ELSTER help on Anlage N-Gre 2024/2025) [verified]:**
- The KTG is tax-free „auf die der Arbeitnehmer einen eigenen Anspruch gegen die Versicherung hat“.
- Opposite case: „Hat der Arbeitgeber das alleinige Forderungsrecht“ → the payout is taxable wages, but then the premiums are not taxable.
- Section lines 94–96: employer contributions to collective KTG where the employee has an independent right to claim „sind steuerpflichtiger Arbeitslohn“; the employee „grundsätzlich“ has an independent right to claim under the KVG or VVG (the guide cites „Artikel 87 VAG“, presumably meaning Art. 87 VVG – spelled this way in the guide).
- „Die Auszahlung solcher Versicherungsleistungen führt nicht zu steuerpflichtigem Arbeitslohn.“
- Payments processed via the employer are „in Zeile 8 der Anlage N‑Gre abzuziehen“ (2025: line 9) if included in item 1 of the Lohnausweis.
  Source 2024: https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2024
  Source 2025: https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2025

→ **The employer paying the premiums does no harm; it is precisely the other side of the coin:** premiums = wages (future provision), benefit = not wages. The benefit only becomes taxable if the employer has the *sole* right to claim (then the premiums are not wages).

**BFH case law:**
- BFH of 11.12.2008, VI R 9/05 (group *accident* insurance, principle transferable) [verified via full-text reproduction on lexetius]: wages on payment of contributions require that the employee „ein unentziehbarer Rechtsanspruch auf die Leistung zusteht“; „Daran fehlt es bei einem nur mittelbaren Anspruch“. https://lexetius.com/2008,3942
- BFH of 26.05.1998, VI R 9/96, BStBl II 1998, 581: insurance benefits on the employee's own claims are regularly not wages [not verified; only quoted in secondary sources, e.g. https://www.iww.de/quellenmaterial/id/21045].
- BFH of 15.11.2007, VI R 30/04: Krankentagegeld from a Swiss employer's collective insurance; remanded to examine whether the employee was (co-)entitled; in essence: if the premiums are already wages, the benefit is not further wages [not verified; reference https://www.anwalt24.de/urteile/bfh/2007-11-15/vi-r-30_04 (403 on retrieval)].
- BFH of 29.04.2009, X R 31/08, BFH/NV 2009, 1625: according to citations, concerns a Swiss health insurer's *birth allowance* and employer future-provision benefits, not a leading KTG case [not verified; https://dejure.org/2009,3371].
- **No BFH ruling found that conclusively decides on the Progressionsvorbehalt (progression proviso) for Swiss collective KTG of Grenzgänger** (see point 2).

## 2. Progressionsvorbehalt?

**Statute [verified]** (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__32b.html):
- Nr. 1 Buchst. b: „Krankengeld, Mutterschaftsgeld, … oder vergleichbare Lohnersatzleistungen“ – only under the *domestic* statutes named there (SGB V etc.).
- Nr. 1 Buchst. k: „nach § 3 Nummer 2 Buchstabe e steuerfreie Leistungen, wenn vergleichbare Leistungen inländischer öffentlicher Kassen“ fall under a–j.

**FG Baden-Württemberg, rulings of 08.05.2019, 14 K 2647/18 and 14 K 1955/18:** collective KTG tax-free, **no Progressionsvorbehalt**; KTG is not part of the statutory benefit scope of Swiss health insurers, benefits from private insurers are not comparable with domestic public funds; employer premiums = wages; the Lohnausweis has no constitutive effect; appeal admitted. [not verified in the primary text; press release FG BW PM 11/2019 at https://finanzgericht-bw.justiz-bw.de/pb/,Lde/Startseite/Presse/PM+11_2019/?LISTPAGE=6601286 now returns 404; content via https://www.stb-web.de/news/article.php/id/23011 and https://www.datev-magazin.de/nachrichten-steuern-recht/steuern/krankentaggelder-einer-schweizer-kollektiv-krankentaggeldversicherung-sind-steuerfrei-und-erhoehen-nicht-den-steuersatz-sog-progressionsvorbehalt-20835]
- Appeals at the BFH: III R 52/19 and III R 56/19 (assignment inconsistent across sources). For III R 56/19 a „Beschluss v. 17.03.2020“ is cited → presumably disposed of without a decision on the merits (withdrawal/discontinuation) **[not verified, outcome unknown]**. Source of the reference: https://datenbank.nwb.de/Dokument/811410/ , rewis entry https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/6hm-08-05-2019-14-k-195518/

**BFH of 24.06.2026, VI R 26/24 [verified]** https://www.bundesfinanzhof.de/de/entscheidung/entscheidungen-online/detail/STRE202610157/
- Headnote 1: the daily allowance of the *statutory* Swiss accident insurance (UVG) is tax-free under § 3 Nr. 2 e in conjunction with Nr. 1 a and „unterliegt daher dem Progressionsvorbehalt“ (Buchst. k).
- Headnote 2: for the Progressionsvorbehalt, what matters is „nur auf die Vergleichbarkeit“ with benefits of domestic public funds; it is irrelevant who finances it and whether a public fund pays.
- The statement of facts mentions an „unstreitiges Krankentaggeld“; the Finanzamt had deducted accident and sickness daily allowance, „berücksichtigte diese aber im Rahmen des Progressionsvorbehalts“. Only the accident daily allowance was in dispute; the BFH says nothing about the KTG.
- **Risk:** The logic of headnote 2 (only functional comparability, financing/provider irrelevant) could be transferred to collective KTG if one classifies it – as the guide does – under § 3 Nr. 2 e rather than directly under § 3 Nr. 1 a (private health insurance). Then Buchst. k would apply and KTG would probably be comparable with Krankengeld. Counter-argument FG BW 2019: private KTG (VVG) is already exempt under § 3 Nr. 1 a, Buchst. k only applies to § 3 Nr. 2 e, Buchst. b only to domestic statutes.

**Official guide [verified]:** For line 8/9 (KTG) there is no reference to line 36 of the main form; by contrast, for UVG daily allowances (line 9/10) and EOG/IV daily allowances it expressly says „zusätzlich in Zeile 36 des Hauptvordrucks einzutragen“. KTG is also not in the list of examples of wage replacement benefits. → The administration apparently sees **no** Progressionsvorbehalt for KTG (inferred from silence, not stated expressly).

## 3. Anlage N-Gre and guide

- Form 2024 line 8 „Steuerfreies Krankentaggeld“ (deduction line „−“); 2025 line 9. Employer contributions to KTG: 2024 line 17, 2025 line 18; employee contributions / taxed employer contributions as Sonderausgaben (special expenses) line 80; collective-contract questions lines 94–96. [verified against the official form PDF and ELSTER help, links above]
- Note: The facts at hand name „Zeile 18“ for the employer premiums for 2024; according to the 2024 guide it is **line 17** (2024 line 18 = NBUV). 2025: line 18. Please check.
- Key sentence (guide 2024): „Daher sind Auszahlungsbeträge, die über den Schweizer Arbeitgeber abgewickelt werden, in Zeile 8 … abzuziehen“.
- **Distinction between continued pay and daily allowance:** The guide expressly distinguishes only by the right to claim (employee's own claim vs. employer's sole right to claim) and only speaks of „Leistungen aus der Krankentaggeldversicherung“. The employer's own continued pay (e.g. waiting period, top-up beyond the daily allowance) is not an insurance benefit and is therefore wages under general principles; the guide contains **no** express statement on this.

## 4. Result

**Tax-free, deduct in Anlage N-Gre 2024 line 8 (20.843 CHF converted), without Progressionsvorbehalt** (no entry in line 36 of the main form).

- Tax exemption: **high certainty** (official guide explicit, BFH principles on the employee's own legal claim, employer premiums already recorded as wages).
- No Progressionsvorbehalt: **medium certainty** (the guide is deliberately silent in contrast to UVG/EOG/IV; FG BW 2019 expressly against the Progressionsvorbehalt; but no BFH ruling; VI R 26/24 headnote 2 provides arguments for a Progressionsvorbehalt; in practice some Finanzämter have applied it, cf. statement of facts VI R 26/24).

**What would change the answer:**
1. The employer has the **sole right to claim** against the insurer (no own claim of the employee) → the payment is wages, do not deduct; but then do **not** add the employer premiums as wages either.
2. The 20.843 CHF include **continued pay/top-up by the employer** (waiting period, difference to 100 %) → this part remains wages; only the amount coming from the insurer is deductible (request the insurer's statement).
3. The daily allowance actually comes from the **UVG** (accident, SUVA or another UVG insurer) instead of KTG → tax-free, but **with Progressionsvorbehalt** (line 9 + line 36 of the main form), BFH VI R 26/24.
4. A future BFH ruling or BMF letter on collective KTG that transfers VI R 26/24 headnote 2 → Progressionsvorbehalt.
