# Insurer 2 basic insurance 2025: deductible, and where?

Research as of: 22.11.2026. Assessment period 2025. Facts: married couple resident in Baden-Württemberg, both Grenzgänger (cross-border commuters) to Switzerland, taxable in Germany, one child. Both are insured only in the Swiss KVG basic insurance, without GKV and without an employer subsidy. In July 2025 they switched insurer:

| Person | Insurer 1 Jan–Jun (CHF) | Insurer 2 Jul–Dec (CHF) | Year (CHF) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parent A | 2.115,00 | 1.477,80 | 3.592,80 |
| Parent B | 2.115,00 | 1.477,80 | 3.592,80 |
| Child | 522,60 | 381,60 | 904,20 |
| Total | 4.752,60 | **3.337,20** | 8.089,80 |

The previous tax return contained only the Insurer 1 contributions.

---

## Short answer

**It is correct to add the Insurer 2 premiums.** The contributions to both insurers paid in 2025 belong together in the annual total. There is no rule that prohibits this. Confidence: high (approx. 90 %).

Two points should still be checked:
1. **Exchange rate:** If you recompute the annual average from the official BMF monthly rates for 2025 yourself, you get **1,0671 EUR/CHF**, not 1,065. The difference is about 7 EUR for the Insurer 2 premiums and about 17 EUR for the annual total (section 5).
2. **What the document covers:** Only basic insurance premiums may be included. VVG supplementary insurance, Franchise and Selbstbehalt (deductible and co-payment) do not belong to the basic cover (section 1d).

---

## 1. Deductibility as basic health insurance (§ 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 Satz 1 Buchst. a EStG)

### a) Statute

- Health insurance contributions are deductible to the extent they are required for a level of care equivalent to social assistance („sozialhilfegleichen Versorgungsniveaus erforderlich“). Source: § 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 Satz 1 Buchst. a EStG, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__10.html
- Contributions to the basic cover are deductible in full. The caps under § 10 Abs. 4 EStG do not limit them. According to the BMF, a „betragsmäßige Deckelung … nicht“ takes place (BMF 24.05.2017, Rz. 130, reference below). According to the ELSTER guide 2025 they are „in voller Höhe berücksichtigt“: https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2025
- **Requirement 1: no connection with tax-free income** (§ 10 Abs. 2 Satz 1 Nr. 1 EStG). The wages of Grenzgänger are taxed in Germany. The deduction ban is therefore not affected from the outset. The counter-exception for EU/EEA/Switzerland and the BMF letter of 03.04.2025 only concern cases with **tax-free** income and play no role here. Sources: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__10.html and BMF 03.04.2025, IV C 4 - S 2221/00380/003/005: https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Einkommensteuer/2025-04-03-sonderausgabenabzug-vorsorgeaufwen.pdf
- **Requirement 2: the recipient** (§ 10 Abs. 2 Satz 1 Nr. 2 EStG). Eligible are insurance companies with their seat in the EU or EEA or with a license to do business in Germany (Buchst. a) as well as a „Sozialversicherungsträger“ (Buchst. c). Switzerland is **not** named in Buchst. a. Source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__10.html
  - The BMF letter of 24.05.2017, Rz. 97, provides: if it is shown that the recipient is a foreign social insurance provider, the contributions are deductible under § 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 Satz 1 Buchst. a in conjunction with Abs. 2 Satz 1 Nr. 2 Buchst. c EStG. A confirmation from the state or an umbrella association is sufficient as evidence. Source: BMF 24.05.2017, IV C 3 - S 2221/16/10001 :004, Rz. 97, copy at the BayLfSt: https://www.lfst.bayern.de/fileadmin/RESSOURCEN/INFORMATIONEN/Steuerinfos/Weitere_Themen/Alterseinkuenfte/2017-05-24-einkommensteuerrechtliche-behandlung-von-vorsorgeaufwendungen.pdf
  - Insurer 1 and Insurer 2, as licensed health insurers, carry out the social health insurance under the KVG. They therefore fit Buchst. c. **Not verified:** I did not find a primary source that expressly classifies Swiss KVG insurers as „Sozialversicherungsträger“ within the meaning of § 10 Abs. 2 Satz 1 Nr. 2 Buchst. c EStG.
- **Not found: a dedicated BMF letter on KVG premiums.** There is no BMF letter that expressly says „KVG = Basisabsicherung“ or sets a percentage reduction for KVG premiums. The country table for global contributions (Rz. 205 of the BMF letter of 24.05.2017) concerns uniform social insurance contributions. KVG per-capita premiums, however, are levied separately, so the table does not apply here.
- **Indirect confirmation from case law:** The FG Baden-Württemberg treated Swiss NBUV premiums like basic health insurance to the extent that they cover medical treatment costs. Ruling of 26.11.2020, 3 K 3139/19, appeal X R 1/21. This information comes only from a secondary source: https://blogs.pwc.de/de/steuern-und-recht/article/228389/ I could not verify the outcome of the appeal at the BFH.

### b) The form expressly treats foreign basic health insurance as deductible

- Anlage Vorsorgeaufwand 2025, line 28 (Kz. 333/433), reads: contributions to a foreign health insurance that „vergleichbar ist (nur Basisabsicherung, keine Wahlleistungen)“ with a domestic one. Source: official form, copy at https://www.buhl.de/steuer/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2026/02/Anlage_Vorsorgeaufwand_2025.pdf
- Foreign contributions cannot be transmitted electronically. Therefore the consent under § 10 Abs. 2a EStG is „mangels Datenübermittlung irrelevant“. Evidence is provided „in geeigneter Form“ (BMF 24.05.2017, Rz. 192, reference above). The ELSTER guide to Anlage N-Gre requires a „Nachweis über Beiträge zu ausländischen Krankenversicherungen“ for this: https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2025

### c) 4 % reduction for Krankengeld: not applicable

- Under § 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 Satz 4 EStG the contribution is „um 4 Prozent zu vermindern“, but **only** if the contributions can give rise to an entitlement to Krankengeld (sickness benefit). Sources: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__10.html and BMF 24.05.2017, Rz. 101 and 103.
- The compulsory health care insurance (OKP) under the KVG pays no daily allowance. Daily allowance insurance under the KVG is a separate, **voluntary** product. Source: BAG, https://www.bag.admin.ch/de/krankenversicherung-die-freiwillige-taggeldversicherung
  - **Not verified:** I could not retrieve the wording of Art. 1a and Art. 67 KVG on fedlex.admin.ch because the site only loads with JavaScript.
- An employer's collective Krankentaggeld is recorded separately in Anlage N-Gre: line 80, section 6, lines 94–96. Source: form N-Gre 2025 in https://www.buhl.de/steuer/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2026/06/alle-formulare-2025-2.pdf
- **Consequence:** Line 29 (Kz. 334/434) contains the same amount as line 28, i.e. „kein Anspruch auf Krankengeld“ (no entitlement to sickness benefit). For the child, line 38 of Anlage Kind (Kz. 90) stays empty.

### d) What does not belong

- Optional and supplementary tariffs do not belong to the basic cover (BMF 24.05.2017, Rz. 100). In Switzerland these are the VVG supplementary insurances. They belong in line 33 of Anlage Vorsorgeaufwand (Kz. 339/439) and in practice have virtually no effect on the result, because the basic contributions already exhaust the cap (Rz. 130).
- Self-borne medical costs due to a deductible are not contributions (BMF 24.05.2017, Rz. 82). In Switzerland these are Franchise and Selbstbehalt.
- Switzerland has no separate long-term care insurance. Line 30 therefore stays empty.

## 2. Which lines

### Anlage N-Gre: not for the KVG premiums

- In section 3 of Anlage N-Gre 2025 (lines 75–88), only the contributions „über den ausländischen Arbeitgeber abgerechneten“ are to be entered, i.e. AHV/IV, EO, ALV, NBUV, Krankentaggeld and BVG. The form goes on: „Die übrigen Versicherungsbeiträge tragen Sie bitte in EUR auf der Anlage Vorsorgeaufwand ein“. Source: form N-Gre 2025 (copy in alle-formulare-2025-2.pdf, link above).
- The ELSTER guide to lines 75 to 88 says the same: to be entered are the „Sozialabgaben der Schweiz“ processed via the Swiss employer. Source: https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2025
- The insured persons pay the KVG premiums themselves directly to the insurer. **They therefore belong in Anlage Vorsorgeaufwand.**

### Anlage Vorsorgeaufwand 2025, sub-page 4 „Beiträge zur ausländischen gesetzlichen oder privaten Kranken- und Pflegeversicherung“

| Line | Content | Kz. A / B | Entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| 28 | Basic health insurance, comparable with a domestic one (without optional benefits) | 333 / 433 | annual total Insurer 1 + Insurer 2 per parent in EUR |
| 29 | of which: contributions without entitlement to Krankengeld | 334 / 434 | the same amount as in line 28 |
| 30 | Long-term care insurance, comparable with a domestic one | 335 / 435 | empty |
| 31–32 | refunded contributions | 336–337 / 436–437 | only if something was refunded |
| 33 | beyond the basic cover (supplementary insurance) | 339 / 439 | VVG parts, if any |

Sources: form (buhl copy, link above). According to the ELSTER guide, contributions without entitlement to Krankengeld belong in „Zeilen 12, 15, 29 oder 32“, and foreign contributions on „Teilseite 4“. Link: https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2025

Note: Several advice websites name lines 31–36 for 2025. That is the numbering of earlier years and wrong for the 2025 form.

### Anlage Kind 2025: line 37, Kz. 89

- Line 37 (Kz. 89) is headed „Beiträge zur ausländischen Kranken- und Pflegeversicherung“. Meant are contributions that „vergleichbar sind (nur Basisabsicherung)“ with a domestic statutory insurance and that were „mir / uns / dem Kind als Versicherungsnehmer geschuldet und von mir / uns getragen“.
- Contributions beyond the basic cover belong in line 30 (Kz. 69). Line 38 (Kz. 90) is for the portion with entitlement to Krankengeld and stays empty here.
- Source: form Anlage Kind 2025 (copy in alle-formulare-2025-2.pdf, link above). The ELSTER guide says: enter contributions to a foreign insurance „in die Zeilen 37 und 38“, provide evidence, and give the child's Steuer-ID (tax identification number), because it is a prerequisite (link above).
- The child's contributions do **not** additionally belong in Anlage Vorsorgeaufwand. The form makes this clear with „(Nicht in der Anlage Vorsorgeaufwand enthalten)“, and it follows from the principle of single consideration (BMF 24.05.2017, Rz. 81).

## 3. Both insurers in the same year: add them up

- The outflow principle applies: „Ausgaben sind für das Kalenderjahr abzusetzen, in dem sie geleistet worden sind.“ Source: § 11 Abs. 2 Satz 1 EStG, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__11.html. All basic contributions paid in 2025 count, no matter to which insurer.
- There is no provision in § 10 EStG that limits the deduction to one insurer (link above). Restrictions exist only for simultaneous **double** basic cover, e.g. GKV plus private comprehensive insurance (BMF 24.05.2017, Rz. 83). Here, however, the cover is consecutive (Jan–Jun, then Jul–Dec), not concurrent.
- For a change of insurer during the year, the contractually owed contributions of all insurers concerned are to be included („aller betroffenen Versicherungsunternehmen einzubeziehen“) (BMF 24.05.2017, Rz. 140). The paragraph concerns the advance payment volume, but presupposes exactly this summation.
- To check: the Insurer 1 document must really cover only January to June, so that nothing is counted twice. In addition, the July premium to Insurer 2 must have been paid in 2025, not as a prepayment in 2024.

## 4. Result

**Adding the Insurer 2 premiums of 3.337,20 CHF is correct.** Confidence: approx. 90 %. The remaining uncertainty concerns the formal classification of the Swiss insurers as „Sozialversicherungsträger“ under § 10 Abs. 2 Satz 1 Nr. 2 Buchst. c EStG, which is not backed by a primary source. The official form expressly provides for foreign basic health insurance, and the Finanzämter in Baden-Württemberg know this group of cases well. The uncertainty is therefore low in practice.

## 5. Exchange rate

- What counts is the „Jahresdurchschnitt der monatlich festgesetzten“ VAT conversion rates (BMF 24.05.2017, Rz. 98).
- The official BMF rates for 2025 in CHF per 1 EUR are: 0,9414 · 0,9413 · 0,9548 · 0,9370 · 0,9356 · 0,9380 · 0,9325 · 0,9387 · 0,9350 · 0,9289 · 0,9290 · 0,9332. Source: https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Datenportal/Daten/offene-daten/steuern-zoelle/umsatzsteuer-umrechnungskurse/umsatzsteuer-umrechnungskurse.html (dataset uu-kurse-2025-csv)
- The average is 0,93712 CHF/EUR, which corresponds to **1,0671 EUR/CHF**. I computed this value myself. I did not find an annual average value published by the BMF.
- Where the value 1,065 comes from is unclear. For the amounts here the difference is less than 20 EUR.
